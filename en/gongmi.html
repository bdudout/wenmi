A partial critical edition of nine telegrams

Nine telegrams of June 1916 (Huang Xing, Zhang Ji and the Shanghai group) can be read, at least in part, with candidate key C51; N07 remains unread. Uncertain characters remain marked.[25]

A separate historical system: the “共密” telegrams

These messages do not use the G0 and Y0 grids described above. The clear telegraph prefix 共密 distinguishes them; the prefix alone does not prove that every message bearing it used one shared historical key.

Candidate model C51 reads the digit groups as follows: traverse each form by columns from right to left, top to bottom, then continue onto the next sheet; remove the public prefix 0364 1378 once; replace the tens digit using the table below; calculate P = (9000 − normalized C) mod 10000 with ordinary borrowing; then look up P in the Chinese Telegraph Code (CTC).

φ(C)9761405283

This rule yields interpretable Chinese passages in N17 and N18. It remains a candidate reconstruction: the historical codebook used in 1916 has not been identified. One printed CTC edition matches 14 targeted entries, which does not establish that it was the edition in use at the time. The partial edition of N17 and N18 presents the scans and reading uncertainties.

A programme following Li's accession: constitution, Parliament and cabinet. Several terms, a loan condition and repeated characters remain unresolved.

Raw decoding, without repairs

Translation

President Li has taken office and the general situation is beginning to settle. In my humble view, we must first calm people’s minds and remove coercion. For the most important and urgent matters, please consult secretly with Jiwu, Zu’an and the others, and quickly ask Mr Li to carry out: 1. restore the old Provisional Constitution and the Parliament; 2. issue no amnesty and no order dropping prosecutions; 3. make Duan and the others resign and appoint anew a provisional premier, without readmitting cabinet members who helped Yuan’s rebellion, then, once Parliament meets, form a regular cabinet anew; 4. transfer Wang Zhanyuan and appoint as governor a commander of the people’s army holding strong forces; 5. invite the leading men of the people’s party to Beijing to consult on everything. Moreover, the Japanese authorities have decided not to lend to the Beijing government unless North and South are unified. So Mr Li should not be constrained. For information. — Xing. The 10th.

From Shanghai: Beijing’s manoeuvres over the Constitution, Huang Qun in Beijing; the Shanghai parliamentarians object; wait and see.

Raw decoding, uncorrected

馮極暗昧又京電段等議決各省派三人赴京議約法滬放客早知梁陰謀詬約法換得內閣段爲總統黃羣到京朞段變滬議員卽開會反對不恢復舊約法恐纍戰公暫靜觀會城

Proposed reading

馮極暗昧。又京電：段等議決各省派三人赴京議約法。滬{政}客早知梁陰謀，{詢}約法，換得內閣，段爲總統。黃羣到京，〔朞〕段變，滬議員卽開會反對。不恢復舊約法，恐〔纍〕戰，公暫靜觀。〔會城〕

Translation

Feng is very ambiguous. Another telegram from Beijing: Duan and the others have decided that each province will send three men to Beijing to discuss the Constitution. The Shanghai politicians long knew Liang’s schemes: [to] the Constitution, obtain the cabinet in exchange, with Duan as president. Huang Qun has arrived in Beijing; […] Duan has changed; the Shanghai members of Parliament will meet at once to oppose it. If the old Constitution is not restored, we fear fighting. Watch quietly for now. […]

北京召集各省代噸議約法意在消滅約沮伋圄會請凌同人逶定安全地方自行鰭會並設法説獨立各省不派代噸蓷鹽款仍未交現黏不自由段有襲嚥故智之勢宜由圄會議嗹與民軍外交代噸奡言請外人俟正統一後再交興

北京召集各省代{表}議約法，意在消滅約{法}〔伋〕{國}會。請〔凌〕同人{速}定安全地方自行{開}會，並設法説獨立各省不派代{表}。〔蓷〕鹽款仍未交，現〔黏〕不自由。段有襲{袁}故智之勢，宜由{國}會議〔嗹〕，與民軍外交代{表}〔奡〕言，請外人俟正統一後再交。興

Translation

Beijing is summoning delegates of the provinces to discuss the Constitution, intending to destroy the Constitution and the Parliament.
