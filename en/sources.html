Sources and credits

Numbers match the note calls in the articles and the Gongmi edition. Web pages were consulted between 4 and 10 October 2026.

This investigation builds first on the article and repository by Daniel Bourdeau [1, 2] (transcriptions, decipherments, code), published under CC BY 4.0. The March–May 1916 texts have been re-read on the scans: see The 1916 condenser telegrams revisited.

It also owes a great deal to the work of S. Tomokiyo on his site Cryptiana [8, 9], devoted to the history of cryptography, Chinese and Japanese in particular: Yamada Junzaburō's “condenser” tables, and the references of the 1916 Japanese reports on the reading of Sun Yat-sen's telegrams (JACAR B03050730500, B03050738800).

Daniel Bourdeau also checked the G0 and Y0 grids independently, with his own transcriptions of the forms (0416b, 0489a), and pointed out to us that the 文密 prefix belongs to the Military Affairs Council channel only.

Our thanks to both.

  8. JACAR, same series, vol. 2: B03050088000 (17 February – 9 March 1916), B03050088100 (10 – 22 March) and B03050088200 (23 – 28 March 1916): condenser telegrams earlier than those transcribed by D. Bourdeau.
  9. JACAR, Diplomatic Archives of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan, series “特定人ノ発受郵便物取調”, vol. 1: B03050087300, B03050087400, B03050087500 (1913–1914); vol. 2: B03050088500, B03050088600 (April 1916). Unpublished telegrams, 1913–1916: transcriptions by Claude incorporating Brice Dudout’s non-blind review; edition and publication checks with Codex. The Shanghai, Qingdao, San Francisco and Manila tables used in this batch were published by D. Bourdeau [1]; Laisaosang and New York were recovered in this investigation, as were Sixteenth, Seventeenth, Hsuweilin and Hong Kong. Provenance manifest: transcriptions, alignment hypotheses, selection criteria and SHA-256 hashes of readings and human reviews. Original PDFs are available through their JACAR references.
  10. JACAR B03050730500: report 政機密第26号 from the consul-general in Hankou, 瀬川淺之進, to Minister 石井菊次郎, 9 May 1916 ("−111" method).
  11. JACAR B03050738800: report 政機第28号, same author, 11 May 1916 ("reverse the four digits, then −111" method), with copies of the April 1916 telegrams.
  12. JACAR, Diplomatic Archives of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan, series 1.6.1.4-2-1-2 “特定人ノ発受郵便物取調”, vol. 3: B03050089300 (digitized PDF, pp. 16 recto–25 verso; forms transcribed as N17 and N18).
